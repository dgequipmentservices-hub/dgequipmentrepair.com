About

DG Equipment Repair

Founded by Danny Gustus · Est. 2022

Founded in 2022, DG Equipment Repair is a mobile equipment repair operation based out of Oceanside, NY. With over 20 years of hands-on experience across gas, diesel, hydraulics and small engines, we've worked on everything from residential lawn equipment to heavy commercial iron.

We built this business around one idea: your equipment gets fixed where it sits. No shop drop-offs, no waiting in line, no dealing with someone who's never seen your machine before. We come to you, get it done, and get out of your way.

We run fully stocked trucks so most repairs are finished in a single visit. We work with homeowners, landscapers, contractors and facilities managers across Nassau County. No job too small, no machine too big.
