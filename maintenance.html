What's Covered

Every Visit Includes

Every scheduled maintenance visit covers the full checklist — nothing skipped, nothing upsold. We bring the parts, we do the work, and we document everything. If we find something that needs more than routine service, we flag it before it becomes an emergency.

Consumables are included in the visit. Filters, blades, oil, spark plugs, belts — we stock the truck for your equipment and replace on schedule. No separate orders, no waiting on parts.

  • Oil & Filter ChangeFresh oil every service interval, filter replaced every visit.
  • Air Filter Inspection & ReplacementChecked and swapped out on schedule — critical for engine life.
  • Fuel System InspectionLines, filters, carburetors — checked and cleaned as needed.
  • Spark Plug ServiceInspected and replaced on interval. No misfires, clean starts.
  • Blade & Cutting Deck ServiceBlades inspected, sharpened, or replaced. Deck cleaned and checked.
  • Belt & Drive InspectionAll drive and accessory belts checked for wear and tension.
  • Battery & Electrical CheckTerminals cleaned, battery load tested, connections inspected.
  • Full Operational TestEvery piece of equipment is run before we leave.

All consumables are sourced, carried, and installed by us — no separate purchase orders, no waiting, no back-and-forth. For commercial landscapers running multiple machines, this alone saves hours every month.
